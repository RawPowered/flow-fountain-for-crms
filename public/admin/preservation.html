Protection posture

Control-plane guarantees

  • Credentials are encrypted and referenced by ID.
  • Definitions are declarative, versioned, and immutable after publication.
  • Jobs use validated state transitions and idempotency keys.
  • Artifacts require SHA-256 verification and delivery evidence.
  • Local staging never qualifies as off-instance protection.
